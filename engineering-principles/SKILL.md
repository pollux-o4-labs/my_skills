# Apply software engineering principles

Consider the principles broadly. Select and combine the approaches that best fit the task, including perspectives that may reveal design blind spots or enable useful reframing.

SOLID, GRASP, GoF Object-Oriented Design Patterns, KISS, YAGNI, DRY, Separation of Concerns, High Cohesion, Low Coupling, Law of Demeter, Information Hiding, Open/Closed Principle.

# Scope

- Implementation and refactoring
- Architecture and module boundaries
- Responsibility, dependency, and extension decisions
