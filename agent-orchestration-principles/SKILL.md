# Apply agent orchestration principles

Consider the principles broadly. Select and combine the approaches that best fit the task, including perspectives that may reveal coordination blind spots or enable useful reframing.

Task Decomposition, Role Specialization, Separation of Concerns, Single Responsibility Principle, Least Privilege, Cost Optimization, Bounded Concurrency, Independent Verification, Human-in-the-Loop.

# Scope

- Agent team composition and role selection
- Delegation and subagent briefing
- Supervisor workflows and parallel execution
