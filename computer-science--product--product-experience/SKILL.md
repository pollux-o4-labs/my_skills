# Apply product and experience principles

Consider the principles broadly. Select and combine the approaches that best fit the task, including perspectives that may reveal blind spots or enable useful reframing.

Product Discovery: Customer Development, Jobs to Be Done, Problem-Solution Fit, Value Proposition, Hypothesis-Driven Product Discovery, Lean Experimentation, Assumption Mapping, Opportunity Solution Tree, Evidence-Informed Product Prioritization, Product-Market Fit.
Product Design: Human-Centered Design, User-Centered Design, Desirability, Feasibility, Viability, Prototyping, Usability Testing, Progressive Disclosure, Affordance, Visibility of System Status, Error Prevention, Inclusive Design.
UX Design: User Research, Information Architecture, Interaction Design, Mental Models, Cognitive Load, Task Analysis, Task Flows, User Flows, Heuristic Evaluation, Accessibility, Service Blueprinting.
Design Systems: Design Tokens, Design System Foundations, Component Library, Design Patterns, Accessibility, Component API, Governance, Contribution Model, Documentation, Semantic Versioning, Visual Regression Testing.
Content Design: Content Strategy, UX Writing, Microcopy, Plain Language, Information Scent, Voice and Tone, Error Messaging, Progressive Disclosure, Inclusive Language, Localization, Content Usability Testing.
Product Management: Product Strategy, Outcome-Based Roadmaps, Product Discovery, Prioritization, Opportunity Cost, Minimum Viable Product (MVP), Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), Product Success Metrics, Stakeholder Alignment, Risk Management, Product Decision Log.

# Scope

- Product discovery, design, experience, content, and planning
- User value, usability, accessibility, design systems, product decisions, and experiments
