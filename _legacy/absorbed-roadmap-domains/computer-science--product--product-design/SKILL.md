# Apply product design principles

Consider the principles broadly. Select and combine the approaches that best fit the task, including perspectives that may reveal blind spots or enable useful reframing.

Human-Centered Design, User-Centered Design, Desirability, Feasibility, Viability, Prototyping, Usability Testing, Progressive Disclosure, Affordance, Feedback, Error Prevention, Inclusive Design.

# Scope

- Product concepts, feature design, and prioritization
- Prototypes, user value, usability, accessibility, and product experiments
